CIWA-Ar — Alcohol Withdrawal Assessment

Clinical Institute Withdrawal Assessment for Alcohol, Revised • Max score: 67

⚠ Clinical Use

CIWA-Ar should be administered regularly (e.g., every 4–8 hours). Use in conjunction with full clinical assessment.

  • Pharmacotherapy is generally recommended for scores ≥8–10
  • Scores ≥15 indicate severe withdrawal requiring urgent treatment
  • Scores ≥20 indicate high risk of seizure and delirium tremens (DTs)
